## 12. Heads-up: CentOS Stream 9 & 10

As of now, we still recommend using CentOS Stream 9 for this course!

CentOS Stream 10 is out and available for download. We have tested it and so far it functions almost identical to CentOS Stream 9.
One of the difference is, that the subscription-manager is enabled by default. And since we don't need to access repositories or updates that need a subscription, we can deactivate it.
Another thing is that programs we install and use in this course, like cowsay, are not available in the repository for CentOS Stream 10 yet. But those will probably be added soon.
Still, for now we recommend using CentOS Stream 9!
